# Chess.com-style play layout

- At 900px and wider, the board is height-limited to fit the viewport, with Black above, Red below, and a 280px game-controls rail alongside. On narrower screens the board fills the width and the controls follow the lower player bar; Zoom/Fit retains horizontal board scrolling.
- Player bars show removed opposing-color pieces, with accessible identities and reveal-on-capture labels. Normally these are the capturer's enemy pieces. The existing capture history does not identify the capturer, so a self-captured covered piece appears on the other color's bar as removed material, not as a claim about who captured it.
- Turn, check, connection and outcome information stays compact; a single live game status also announces captures. End turn and rematch remain available when appropriate. Online invitations and rules remain in their sheets. Red stays at the bottom for both online seats.
- Verification: `npm test` (82 tests) and `npm run build` pass. Chromium viewport checks covered 1440×900, 1024×768, 900×700, 768×1024, 390×844 and 320×568. Touch-emulated mobile checks exercised Zoom/Fit, rules, a capture, reset and End turn; a simulated long capture row was keyboard-scrollable. Two browser sessions exercised room creation, invite/copy, turn gating, reload/reconnect and disconnect pause. A real-device visual check is still recommended.
- Tailnet preview: <http://100.88.150.21:5175/>. Vite is bound to the Tailscale address and uses an isolated room server on localhost port 3016 in the `flip-chesscom-layout` tmux session. The preview is temporary; HTTP browsers without clipboard access can copy the selected invite link manually.
